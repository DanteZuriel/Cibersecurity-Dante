## Industrial Safety to Cybersecurity Glossary
**Glosary for the note [Skills](Skills)**

| Industrial Safety Term (ES) | Cybersecurity Equivalent (EN) | Why it translates this way / Technical Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Controles de Seguridad** | `Security Controls (Administrative & Technical)` | In both fields, controls are the countermeasures put in place to mitigate identified risks and protect assets. |
| **Análisis de Riesgo** | `Risk Assessment / Threat Modeling` | The methodology used to identify vulnerabilities, estimate impact, and implement defensive strategies before an incident occurs. |
| **Normativa STPS** | `Regulatory Compliance (STPS / OSHA Frameworks)` | STPS is Mexico's labor standard. Mapping it alongside **OSHA** gives international recruiters immediate context regarding your compliance experience. |
| **Métodos de Trabajo** | `Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Policies` | Documenting strict, safe workflows translates directly into writing security policies, hardening guides, and operational playbooks. |
| **Prevención de Incendios** | `Disaster Recovery (DR) & Business Continuity` | Fire prevention plans are designed to ensure physical survival and continuity; in tech, DR plans ensure the company survives a catastrophic data breach. |
| **Planes de Emergencia** | `Incident Response (IR) Planning` | Knowing exactly who to call, what to shut down, and how to isolate a threat during a physical crisis is the exact logic used in a cyberattack containment. |
| **Auditoría Documental** | `Compliance Auditing & GRC` | Reviewing logs, signatures, and paperwork to pass official inspections is the core foundation of **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)**. |
